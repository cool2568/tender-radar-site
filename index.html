Tender Radar

Tender Radar is a Chrome extension for suppliers who bid on GeM (bidplus.gem.gov.in). It finds the ongoing bids that match your keywords and pulls the key fields out of each bid document, so you can decide which bids to pursue without reading every PDF.

What you get

  • Every matching GeM bid in one listMatching bid cards are highlighted on the page you are browsing, and "Scan all of GeM" searches all ongoing bids for your keywords and lists each match once, soonest deadline first.
  • The bid document's 20 key fields as a row in your own Google SheetEnd date, EMD amount, turnover and experience requirements, MSE and startup relaxations and more are read from the bid PDF and written to a spreadsheet in your Google Drive.
  • Nothing leaves your computerBid PDFs are read inside your browser. There is no Tender Radar server: your keywords and bids are never sent to us.

How it works

  1. Install the extension, open the side panel and type the keywords you bid on (for example, solar, battery, transformer).
  2. Open bidplus.gem.gov.in. Matching bids are highlighted; click "Scan all of GeM" to search every ongoing bid.
  3. Click "Extract" on a bid to read its 20 key fields from the bid document.
  4. Click "Connect Google Sheets" once, then "Send to Sheet" to add the bid as a row. Sent bids are tracked, and Chrome reminds you 48 hours and 24 hours before they close.

Privacy

Tender Radar has no server and no analytics. It talks only to GeM, from your own browser, and to Google, to write to the one spreadsheet it creates in your Drive. It asks Google for the narrowest Drive permission, which covers only files the extension created. Read the full privacy policy.
